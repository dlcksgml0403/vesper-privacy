Privacy Policy

Vesper · based on v1.0.0

Effective date: 2026-10-07

1How the current version handles data

  • The current version (v1.0.0) runs entirely on your device — no servers, no accounts.
  • Your baby's profile (name, date of birth, feeding method) and usage logs are processed in the app's memory only and are never transmitted to or stored on our servers.
  • Deleting the app, or using "Delete account & data" in Settings, removes everything you entered.

2Microphone use (cry detection)

  • Cry detection in the current version is a UI simulation: tapping "Start detection" changes the status after about 2.5 seconds. It does not record, analyze, transmit, or store any microphone audio.
  • Automatic cry detection will arrive in a post-launch update. Even then, audio will be processed in real time on your device only — never sent to or stored on servers.
  • The microphone is used only with your explicit permission, and you can turn it off anytime in Settings.

3AI guide questions

  • The current version generates parenting guides from on-device local templates. Text you type into the guide chat is not sent to any external server.
  • If we connect an AI server in the future, we will update this policy first and announce it in the app before it takes effect.

4Subscriptions & payments

  • The current version has no subscription billing yet, so we do not collect or store any payment information.
  • When in-app purchase is introduced, payments will be processed via the Apple App Store / Google Play under each store's policies.
  • Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the trial or billing period ends. Manage or cancel anytime in your device's account settings.

5Third-party services

  • The current version uses no external backend, analytics, or crash-reporting SDKs.
  • If we adopt services such as Supabase, RevenueCat, PostHog, or Sentry, we will revise this policy and notify you in advance.
  • Beyond that, we do not sell or share your personal information with third parties.

6Retention & deletion

  • Data is processed on your device only and is not kept on any server.
  • Deleting the app, or using "Delete account & data" in Settings, immediately removes the information you entered.

7Children

  • This app is a tool for parents and caregivers, not directed at children. Baby information is limited to what a caregiver enters.

8Your rights

  • You may request access, correction, deletion, or restriction of your data via in-app Settings ("Delete account & data") or the contact below.

9Contact

This policy may be updated due to legal changes; material changes will be announced in the app.

10Pre-registration email collection (landing page)

  • When you pre-register for launch updates, we collect your email address.
  • Purpose: sending launch notifications and pre-registration emails. Your email will not be used for any other purpose.
  • Storage location: a cloud storage location designated by the operator. The specific storage method will be stated in this policy once finalized.
